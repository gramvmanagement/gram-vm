ABOUT GRAM MANAGEMENT

Who
I Am

Hello! I’m Gram, the founder and talent operator behind Gram Management.

I am an organized, attentive, and operational-minded advocate for VTubers. While my core focus is rooted in project organization and real-time support, my mission is simple: giving creators a reliable partner in their corner so they never have to navigate the streaming industry alone.

THE BEGINNING

Why I Started
Gram Management

01

A Simple Observation

This agency started from a simple, frustrating observation: VTubers—both independent and corporate—are constantly mishandled, overworked, or left to burn out without support.

02

The Problem

Seeing talented creators struggle with mismanaged launch pipelines, chaotic schedules, unvetted business opportunities, or a total lack of empathy from behind-the-scenes management was hard to watch.

03

The Standard

I realized that the industry doesn't just need more "managers"; it needs authentic talent advocates who prioritize the human being behind the avatar.

Gram Management was created to be the standard I wanted to see: zero corporate coldness, total operational transparency, and genuine 1-on-1 support.
